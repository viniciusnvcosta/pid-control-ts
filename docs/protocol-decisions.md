| D6 | Evaluation grid and missing data. The grid is the weeks where the US forecast and truth are complete. A missing leaf quantile is filled causally with LOCF, and the count of imputed cells goes in the manifest. Truth is never imputed. The last 3–4 weeks have no actuals and are dropped. |
| D6a | Leading weeks in which any leaf has never been forecast are trimmed, since LOCF has nothing to carry. |
| D7 | No look-ahead. The MinT covariance for target t uses only median errors of targets s ≤ `forecast_date(t)` (in practice s ≤ t − 28 days), on an expanding window. The first `mint_warmup = 20` weeks are excluded from evaluation **for every arm**, so all arms share one window. |
| D7a | Every arm, controller and metric starts at `evaluation_start(bundle, mint_warmup)`, and controllers are re-initialized there. |
| D8 | Quantile crossing after MinT is handled by sorting the three levels per node and week. The number of crossings is recorded. |
| D9 | Truth for evaluation is the **reported US actual**, which is what the paper would score. The gap between truth and the coherent sum is logged as a diagnostic. |
| D10 | **Primary endpoint:** mean \|rolling coverage(w = 10) − 0.8\| on the US series. Primary contrast: `mint_shrink` vs `none` under PI at lr = 0.1, the paper's COVID lr. |
| D11 | **Secondary endpoints:** `bottom_up` vs `none`; longest miscoverage run (the paper's metric); marginal coverage; mean and median width; 80% interval score. Holm-corrected. |
| D12 | Statistics: paired moving-block bootstrap over weeks with block = 8 (sensitivity 4 and 12), B = 2000, a single `root_seed` fed to `SeedSequence`. Sensitivity also covers the lr grid {1, 0.5, 0.1, 0.05}. |
| D13 | `pid_theta` runs in a temporary working directory, so core's `.cache/scorecaster` is never reused across runs. |
| D14 | The upstream Theta scorecaster in `core/methods.py:250` assigned the whole `ThetaModel.forecast(ahead)` Series into a scalar slot and crashed under current numpy/pandas for every `ahead`. It now takes the ahead-step value, `model.forecast(ahead).iloc[-1]`. This is the only change to `core/` besides `np.infty` → `np.inf`, and both are pinned by `tests/unit/test_core_characterization.py`. |

## 2026-09-29 (continued)

| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| D15 | **Upstream properties kept, documented.** (1) core's proportional learning rate `lr_t = lr * range(scores[t-T_burnin:t])` sets `q[t+1]` using scores up to `t-1` while only scores up to `t-ahead+1` are known — ~2 weeks of look-ahead in the step size for PI/PID at `ahead = 4`; inherited from the paper, identical across arms so paired contrasts stay fair, but PI/PID are not strictly causal in the lr scale. (2) the Theta scorecaster trains on `scores[:t_pred]` and stores `forecast(ahead)` at index `t + ahead`, so the scorecast used for target τ is a forecast of `s[τ−4]` from data to `τ−8` (2·ahead stale); PID-Theta results are labelled "paper-core scorecaster (2·ahead stale)" and are not evidence about an aligned PID. (3) evaluation and MinT errors use final revised truth (as in the paper), not as-of vintages. |
| D16 | **Start-up caveat on the primary endpoint.** Controllers are re-initialised at `evaluation_start` (2021-01-16, winter-wave peak; 112 weeks); the rolling-coverage endpoint is dominated by the start-up transient (PI offsets jump then decay slowly), so the primary contrast reflects transient plus steady state. PI over-coverage (0.90–0.96 vs 0.80) is a property of the controller on a conservative base with 4-week feedback delay, not an adapter bug (wiring pinned by `test_pi_matches_paper_harness_wiring`). |
| D17 | **Reporting clarifications.** Contrasts are computed only at `lr` (`lr_grid` results are descriptive metrics); contrasts at non-primary block lengths are Holm-adjusted inside the secondary family; with `block < rolling_window` the rolling/run metrics straddle block joins in resamples; MinT may produce negative lower quantiles on small leaves (US row unaffected); crossings are counted with relative tolerance 1e-9 and reported separately for the US row (amends D8's count). |

| D19 | **v1 conclusion and post-hoc diagnostics (exploratory, not confirmatory).** The pre-registered primary contrast (`mint_shrink − none`, PI, lr 0.1, US coverage deviation) is +0.0097, 95% CI [0, 0.029], p = 0.18: no improvement. `results/us-hier-v2` is a rerun of `us-hier-v1` at `a3a91e5` with only `run_id` changed (`configs/us_hier_v2.toml`), and every metric is identical. Diagnostics computed afterwards on the same window: (1) **headroom** — an interval whose weeks are covered i.i.d. with probability 0.8 scores 0.0966 in expectation on this endpoint (w = 10), so the base's 0.151 leaves about 0.055 to gain, the size of the CI half-width; (2) **scale, not location** — quantile-wise reconciliation widens the raw US interval (bottom-up +41%, MinT +24%) because summing quantiles treats leaves as comonotone, while the MinT median improves US MAE by only 1.5% (1320 → 1300), and shifting the base interval to the MinT median raises coverage deviation to 0.183; (3) **controller** — warm-starting PI over the pre-evaluation weeks leaves deviation at 0.151 (mean width 6544 vs 4839 raw), so D16's start-up transient explains only part of PI's over-coverage: with the 10% quantile tracker a miss moves the offset about 9× as far as a hit, and feedback arrives 4 weeks late. Reported in `docs/analysis/us-hier-v2.md`. |
| D20 | **Phase 2 primary hypothesis H1 (leaf level).** Forecaster `COVIDhub-4_week_ensemble`, with the data, window, controllers and settings of D1, D4, D6–D8 and D7a. Endpoint: the mean of `coverage_deviation` over the *endpoint leaves* (D25). Primary contrast: `mint_shrink − none` under PI at lr 0.1, paired moving-block bootstrap with block 8 and B = 2000. One week-index draw per (forecaster, block) is shared by every node, which keeps the cross-leaf dependence in the resamples. Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-20-hierarchical-panel-design.md`. |
| D21 | **H2 (key secondary, generalization across forecasters).** A forecaster is eligible when its bundle builds with exactly the 57 nodes of D5; it has at least 60 evaluation weeks after `mint_warmup = 20`; and at most 5% of its leaf cells are imputed by LOCF. A forecaster is a near-duplicate of another when more than 95% of their shared (node, week) cells have all three quantiles equal (`np.isclose`); of a near-duplicate pair the one with more evaluation weeks is kept, ties broken by name order. Applying the rule to `data/deaths.csv` on 2026-10-20 (availability only, no outcome computed) gives 8 forecasters: `CEID-Walk`, `COVIDhub-4_week_ensemble`, `COVIDhub-baseline`, `COVIDhub-ensemble`, `COVIDhub-trained_ensemble`, `JHUAPL-Bucky`, `RobertWalraven-ESG`, `UMass-MechBayes`. Excluded: `COVIDhub_CDC-ensemble` (duplicate of `COVIDhub-ensemble`), `JHU_IDD-CovidSP` (5.2% imputed), `USC-SI_kJalpha` (57 weeks), `epiforecasts-ensemble1` (40 weeks), and forecasters without 57 nodes or without a complete week. For each eligible forecaster, the H1 contrasts are reported at the leaf-mean and US levels on that forecaster's own window. |
| D22 | **Panel arms.** Reconcilers `none`, `bottom_up`, `mint_shrink`, `mint_median` (D26). Controllers `raw` and `pi`. lr grid {1, 0.5, 0.1, 0.05}, with metrics descriptive and contrasts only at lr 0.1 (D17). `pid_theta` is excluded from the panel because its scorecast is 2·ahead stale (D15) and is not evidence about an aligned PID. Comparisons: each of `mint_shrink`, `bottom_up`, `mint_median` against `none`. |
| D23 | **Scale-free secondaries.** Per node, `scaled_mean_width = mean_width / s` and `scaled_interval_score = interval_score / s`, with `s = max(mean |y|, 1)` over the forecaster's evaluation window. `s` is fixed by the full window and not recomputed per resample. Coverage metrics and `longest_miss_run` are unscaled. A level's value is the mean over its nodes. |
| D24 | **Panel statistics.** Seeds: `SeedSequence(root_seed).spawn(n_forecasters + 1)`. Forecaster *k* (configuration order) spawns one child per block length; the last child draws the cross-forecaster summary. Holm: the primary is unadjusted; every other contrast is adjusted within its (block, forecaster, level, controller) family. The cross-forecaster summary (block 8, `coverage_deviation` and `scaled_interval_score`) reports the number of forecasters, how many have a negative estimate, the mean estimate, and a 95% percentile interval from resampling forecasters with replacement (B = `n_boot`, one draw shared by every summary row). It is descriptive only. |
| D25 | **Endpoint leaves.** The territories `as`, `gu`, `mp`, `pr`, `vi` are reconciled but excluded from the leaf-mean levels; they are near-zero series. A node is scored for a forecaster only when its truth is complete on that forecaster's evaluation window (truth is never imputed, D6); otherwise it gets no interval or metric, and its name is recorded in that forecaster's diagnostics (`unscored_nodes`). The endpoint leaves are the 51 states and DC that are scored. |
| D26 | **`mint_median`.** MinT(Shrink) with the D7 causal covariance is applied to the base quantiles, and every level of a node is moved by that node's median shift: `q_k + (MinT(q)_{0.5} − q_{0.5})`. Medians are coherent, spreads equal the base spreads, and levels never cross. It separates the effect of a coherent location from the widening in D19 (2). |
| D27 | **Phase 2 regression anchor.** On `COVIDhub-4_week_ensemble`, the panel's US-node metrics for `none`, `bottom_up`, `mint_shrink` × `raw`, `pi` × lr grid must equal `results/us-hier-v2/metrics.parquet`. Panel bootstrap seeds differ from v1 (D24), so contrasts are not expected to match. |
